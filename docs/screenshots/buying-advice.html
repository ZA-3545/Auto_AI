Should I buy a used car or new one on this budget?
Get advice

Advice

grounded in buying-advice knowledge

Neither choice is universally better — it depends on your budget, how long you plan to keep the car, and how comfortable you are verifying a used car's condition. Buying used often means a lower upfront price and slower depreciation in the first years you own the car, but you inherit unknown maintenance history and wear. Success usually means a realistic inspection before payment, clear ownership papers, and comparing total cost of ownership — not just the asking price. Buying new typically brings warranty coverage, known service history from day one, and the latest features, but costs more upfront and loses value faster in early years. Even with new cars, compare on-road cost and understand warranty and routine service requirements. On a tight budget in Pakistan, many buyers choose a carefully inspected used car; others prefer new for peace of mind. AutoAI presents these trade-offs honestly — it is not pushing you toward either option.
